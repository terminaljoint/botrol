BOTROL / TERMINAL JOINT

Connect your device.

Sign in, connect the ESP32 by USB, configure Wi-Fi, and pair it automatically. BOTROL Cloud issues a unique credential for this device. You never need to copy or type a permanent device token.

1 · Your BOTROL account

Sign in with Google. Your devices will be private to this account.

Checking Firebase configuration...

2 · Connect ESP32 over USB

Use a USB data cable and a recent Chrome or Edge browser. Select the ESP32 serial port, usually Silicon Labs CP210x or USB Serial Device. This automatic pairing flow requires BOTROL firmware v1.0.0 or newer; the previous hardcoded-token firmware does not support it.

Sign in, then connect the ESP32.
Waiting for ESP32 status...

3 · Configure Wi-Fi

Wi-Fi credentials are sent directly over USB and stored in ESP32 NVS. Use a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network.

Nearby networks will be scanned automatically after the ESP32 responds.

4 · Pair this ESP32 with your account

BOTROL creates a one-time pairing code, sends it directly over USB, and exchanges it for a unique device credential. The credential is saved in ESP32 NVS and is never shown in this page.

Sign in and connect the ESP32 to Wi-Fi before pairing.
Pairing codes expire after five minutes and can only be used once. If a device is already paired, revoke it from its owner account before pairing it again.
